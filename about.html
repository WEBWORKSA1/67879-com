About 67879.com

The Lucky Number Lab — making Chinese number culture understandable, useful and fun.

67879 reads liù qī bā qī jiǔ — “smooth, rise, prosper, rise, lasting”. We built a site worthy of that name: the clearest free tools for understanding what numbers mean to Chinese speakers worldwide.

Our principles

  • Transparent: every score shows the digits, combos and patterns behind it.
  • Private: calculations run in your browser.
  • Respectful: we explain culture, we don't sell superstition as science.

Methodology

Scores start at 50 and are adjusted by each digit's traditional value (8 highest, 4 lowest), known combinations (168, 520, 748…), and patterns (repeats, ascending runs, endings, absence of 4/0). Sources include standard references on Chinese numerology and homophones.
