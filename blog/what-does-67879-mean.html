What does 67879 mean in Chinese?

Published 2026-10-01 · 67879.com editorial

Short answer: 67879 reads liù qī bā qī jiǔ (六七八七九). Through homophones it sounds like 溜 smooth → 起 rise → 发 prosper → 起 rise → 久 lasting: a smooth, rising path to prosperity that lasts. Our analyzer gives it 85/100 — Very Lucky.

Digit by digit

  • 6 六 liù — sounds like 溜/流, “smooth, flowing”; in Cantonese luk6 echoes 禄, official fortune.
  • 7 七 qī — sounds like 起 “to rise” and 齐 “together”. It is the one mixed digit (Ghost Month is the 7th lunar month).
  • 8 八 bā — sounds like 发 fā, “to prosper”. The most valued digit in Chinese culture.
  • 7 七 qī — rise again.
  • 9 九 jiǔ — sounds like 久, “long-lasting”; also the emperor's number.

Why 678 matters

6-7-8 is an ascending run. In Chinese number culture consecutive rising digits are read as 步步高 — “rising step by step”, a common New Year blessing. Ending on 79 (起久) suggests a rise that keeps going. Romantically, 七 can stand for 妻 (wife), so 79 is sometimes read “wife forever”.

As a number asset

67879 has no 4 (death) and no 0, the profile Chinese domain investors call “no-4 no-0”. It contains three classic lucky digits (6, 8, 9). It is not a repeating pattern like 88888, so it trades below top patterns but above most 5N names.

Caveats

Meanings depend on dialect and context. Numerology is cultural tradition, not prediction. Use it for meaning, marketing and memorability.
