Chinese lucky numbers: the complete guide

Published 2026-10-01 · 67879.com editorial

Chinese has hundreds of homophones, so numbers are heard as words. That's why phone numbers, plates, floors and even prices are chosen with care.

The lucky ones

  • 8 八 bā ≈ 发 fā prosper. The Beijing Olympics opened at 8:08:08 pm on 8/8/08.
  • 6 六 liù ≈ 溜 smooth. 666 = 六六大顺, “everything goes smoothly”.
  • 9 九 jiǔ ≈ 久 lasting — popular for weddings.
  • 2 — good things come in pairs (双).

The unlucky ones

  • 4 四 sì ≈ 死 sǐ death. Many towers skip floors 4, 14, 24.
  • 250 二百五 = fool. 748 去死吧 = curse. 38 = gossip insult.

Combinations people use

  • 168 一路发 prosperity all the way
  • 518 我要发 I will prosper
  • 520 我爱你 I love you — 20 May is a wedding hot-date
  • 1314 一生一世 forever

Real money

Sichuan Airlines paid CN¥2.33M for 8888-8888; Hong Kong plate “88” fetched HK$11.4M in 2025. Airlines reserve flight numbers like 888 for China routes.
