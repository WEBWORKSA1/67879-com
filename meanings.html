FAQ

What is the luckiest number in Chinese culture?

8 (八 bā), because it sounds like 发 fā — to prosper. Repeated 8s (88, 888, 8888) multiply the effect.

What is the unluckiest number?

4 (四 sì) sounds like 死 sǐ, death. Combinations like 14, 514 and 748 are especially avoided.

Is 7 lucky or unlucky?

Mixed. 七 qī sounds like 起 rise and 齐 together, but the 7th lunar month is Ghost Month and 七 can echo 欺 'deceive'. Context decides.

What does 67879 mean?

liù qī bā qī jiǔ — smooth · rise · prosper · rise · lasting. 678 is an ascending run and 9 adds longevity.
