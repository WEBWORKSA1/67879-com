# 67879.com — Research & Strategy

## 1. What "67879" means in Chinese culture

| Digit | Mandarin | Homophone | Reading |
|---|---|---|---|
| 6 六 | liù | 溜/流 smooth, flowing · Cantonese 禄 fortune | lucky |
| 7 七 | qī | 起 rise · 齐 together · 妻 wife · 欺 deceive | mixed (Ghost Month = 7th lunar month) |
| 8 八 | bā | 发 fā prosper | the luckiest digit |
| 7 七 | qī | 起 rise | mixed |
| 9 九 | jiǔ | 久 long-lasting, imperial | lucky |

**Reading:** liù qī bā · qī jiǔ → "smooth · rise · prosper · rise · lasting".
- **678** is an ascending run, read as 步步高 ("rising step by step").
- **79** (起久 / 妻久) means "a lasting rise" or "wife forever".
- It has no 4 (death) and no 0. It contains three classic lucky digits (6, 8, 9).
- Score in our model: **85/100, Very Lucky**.

### Why numbers carry economic value in China
- The Beijing Olympics opened at 8:08:08 pm on 8/8/08.
- Sichuan Airlines paid CN¥2.33M (~US$280k) for the phone number 8888-8888.
- Hong Kong plate "88" sold for HK$11.4M (2025 auction, SCMP).
- Airlines reserve flight numbers with 8s for China routes, e.g. AC88, UA888, KL888.
- Numeric domains are major Chinese brands: 163.com, 360.cn, 58.com, 4399.com.
- Reported numeric domain sales: 55.com ($2.3M), 114.com ($2.1M), 88888.com ($245k).
- Numbers avoid China's input-method and dialect friction. Only 100,000 5N .com names exist.
- Chinese buyers price these domains by:
  - whether the number has a 4 or a 0 ("no-4 no-0" is the "chip" grade)
  - how many 8/6/9 digits it has
  - its pattern (AAAAA, AABBB, ABABA)

### Position on 67879.com as an asset
It is a liquid, mid-tier 5N "chip" .com, not a top-pattern name. As a parked domain its value is capped at what Chinese wholesale buyers will pay. Turned into a traffic-earning site with its own revenue, it can be worth a multiple of that. That is the thesis of this build.

## 2. Chosen idea: "Lucky Number Lab"
A global, English-first (with Chinese characters and pinyin) **Chinese lucky-number utility and reference hub**.

**Why it is the strongest choice**
1. **Domain fit.** The domain is a number with a lucky meaning, so the site's subject is the domain.
2. **Evergreen search demand from tools.** Queries like "what does 520 mean", "lucky phone number", "is 4 unlucky", "chinese zodiac lucky numbers" and "license plate meaning" keep coming every year.
3. **Programmatic SEO scale.** There is one page per number, and 0–99,999 is 100,000+ URLs.
4. **Calculator pages earn well from ads.** They have high pageviews per session and high engagement.
5. **Many ways to earn from lead generation:**
   - paid readings
   - wedding and opening date selection
   - business naming
   - bulk B2B number selection for developers, car dealers and telecoms
   - a numeric-domain brokerage desk
6. **Low competition at the intersection.** No site combines Mandarin and Cantonese homophone analysis, 5-digit coverage and 5N domain valuation.

**Rejected alternatives**
- **Pure 5N domain marketplace.** It needs liquidity and escrow trust, and juming and 4.cn already dominate it.
- **Generic Chinese news site.** No fit with the domain, and it is ad-only.
- **Lottery-number site.** Regulatory risk and weak AdSense eligibility.
