Why numbers matter

In China, numbers carry a price tag.

Chinese is rich in homophones, so numbers are heard as words. That turns digits into real money:

  • The Beijing Olympics opened on 8/8/08 at 8:08:08 pm.
  • Sichuan Airlines paid CN¥2.33 million for the phone number 8888-8888.
  • Hong Kong plate “88” sold for HK$11.4 million at auction (2025).
  • Numeric domains such as 163.com, 360.cn and 58.com are top Chinese brands; 55.com sold for $2.3M.

FAQ

What does 67879 mean in Chinese?

Read digit by digit in Mandarin, 6-7-8-7-9 is liù qī bā qī jiǔ. By homophone it suggests 溜 smooth · 起 rise · 发 prosper · 起 rise · 久 lasting — a rising staircase (678) that ends on longevity (9). It contains three classic lucky digits (6, 8, 9) and no 4.

Why is 8 lucky and 4 unlucky?

八 bā sounds like 发 fā (to prosper). 四 sì sounds like 死 sǐ (death), so many buildings skip 4th floors and buyers avoid plates or phone numbers with 4.

Is the lucky number score scientific?

No. It is a transparent cultural scoring model based on widely documented Mandarin and Cantonese homophones and known combinations. Use it for education, fun and cultural awareness.
